Station Archive — Moth Hollow Historical Preservation Society

WHLW 88.3 operated in Moth Hollow from 1971 until its license lapse in early 2002. This site was preserved following a request from county archives. Tape recordings, broadcast logs, and staff documents have been partially digitized. Some materials remain inaccessible due to physical damage or administrative restriction.

For inquiries regarding station records, contact the Hollow Creek County Clerk's office. Note: certain incident materials from October 1998 are not available through this archive.
