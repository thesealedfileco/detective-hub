Broadcast irregularities, archive access, and equipment status — week of October 5–14, 1998
1. Purpose

This memo documents a series of technical and operational irregularities observed during the broadcast week of October 5 through October 14, 1998. Items are recorded here for management review and for the station's permanent maintenance log. No formal complaint is being filed at this time; however, given the ongoing police inquiry, I felt it appropriate to create a written record.

2. Broadcast Frequency — Reported Instability

Beginning on or around October 6, the overnight broadcast slot (approximately 8:00 PM – 1:00 AM) exhibited intermittent signal behavior inconsistent with standard operations. Specifically, the primary transmitter TX-1 / 88.300 MHz showed minor carrier drift, measured at approximately ±0.004 kHz during three separate overnight sessions. This falls within FCC tolerance, but is irregular for our equipment, which has historically been stable.

I reviewed the calibration logs for the relevant period. The most recent full calibration on record is dated September 12, 1998. I found no documentation of any interim calibration or adjustment. The source of the drift has not been conclusively identified. It is possible, though unconfirmed, that the instability is related to environmental factors at the relay tower site — temperature fluctuations, or interference from nearby equipment.

I do not believe the signal irregularity represents a broadcast compliance risk at this time. I am scheduling a full system check for the week of October 19.

3. Archive Room — After-Hours Access Log

During a routine check of the archive room on October 15, I noted that the door access log showed two after-hours entries I could not account for through normal scheduling. The entries are as follows:

Date Entry Time Exit Time Badge ID Logged Notes
October 9, 1998 11:38 PM 12:14 AM BADGE-009 Not a standard staff badge assignment. Badge 009 is listed in equipment inventory as "Consulting Access — Unassigned."
October 12, 1998 11:02 PM 11:49 PM BADGE-009 Same badge. Duration: 47 minutes. No work order filed for either visit.

I have not been able to determine who holds or held Badge 009. The badge issuance log for consulting credentials is kept separately by station management and was not available to me at the time of writing. I would ask that management review the issuance records and confirm the identity of the badge holder.

No equipment appears to have been removed from the archive room. However, I did notice that the tape duplication unit — a TASCAM 122 MK III — showed a counter reading inconsistent with the last logged session. The counter difference suggests approximately 35–40 minutes of operation unaccounted for. I cannot confirm whether duplication activity occurred or whether this is a counter error.

4. Tape Inventory — Discrepancy Note

The archive holds master recordings for all broadcast sessions, organized by date and program. Following standard protocol, I conducted a spot inventory on October 15. I found that the master recording for the overnight slot of October 2, 1998 was stored in the incorrect sleeve — it had been filed under an earlier date. Whether this was a misfiling error or reflects some other activity, I cannot say. The tape itself appears undamaged.

I also note that our working copy of the station archive index — previously maintained as a printed binder — has not been updated since early September. The digital index, accessible via the station's internal reference system, should be considered the current record. The online index is maintained at the usual internal address and should reflect current holdings.

5. Recommended Actions

I respectfully request that management take the following steps:

(a)  Confirm the identity of Badge 009 and clarify whether the holder had authorized after-hours access to the archive room during the dates listed above.

(b)  Review the tape duplication unit's activity log, to the extent that log data has been preserved by the unit itself.

(c)  Consider a formal key and badge audit for all archive access credentials, consulting and staff alike.

(d)  Schedule a transmitter inspection prior to the October 19 calibration, in case the frequency irregularity has a mechanical cause.

I am available to discuss any of the above at management's convenience. No urgency is implied — this is provided for the record.
