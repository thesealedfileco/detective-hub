Narrative — Circumstances as Reported by Initial Officer
Subject Naomi Elise Vale was reported missing on October 15, 1998 at approximately 9:14 AM by her employer, WHLW Station Manager Kenneth Pruitt, after she failed to appear for a scheduled programming review. Subject was last confirmed on station premises by technician Evelyn Pike, who stated Vale was present in the broadcast booth at approximately 10:00 PM on October 13, 1998. Vale's scheduled broadcast ran until 1:00 AM. Station logs indicate broadcast continued until 1:07 AM on October 14, after which the station shifted to automated programming. Subject's vehicle was subsequently recovered on Tower Road approximately two miles from the station on October 16, 1998. No evidence of collision or mechanical failure was observed. Personal effects — including a canvas bag, notebook, and cassette player — were not recovered with the vehicle. No body has been recovered.
