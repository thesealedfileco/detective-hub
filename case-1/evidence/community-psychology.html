About the Practice

Dr. Elias Voss, Ph.D.

Dr. Elias Voss has practiced clinical and community psychology in Moth Hollow, West Virginia for over three decades. His work centers on the relationship between environment, behavior, and community identity — exploring how place shapes the interior life of its inhabitants.

Before establishing his private practice, Dr. Voss worked in applied behavioral research and community consultation, contributing to outreach programs across rural Appalachian counties. He has long believed that psychology's most meaningful work happens not in the clinic alone, but in the wider structures of community life.

His therapeutic approach is integrative and patient-led. He draws from cognitive-behavioral, psychodynamic, and narrative frameworks, adapting to each individual's needs. He works with adults navigating anxiety, grief, life transitions, and long-term psychological patterns. He also consults with local organizations and institutions seeking to better understand and serve their communities.

Dr. Voss is currently accepting a limited number of new patients. Please contact the practice for availability.

Clinical Services

How We Can Help

Individual Therapy

One-on-one sessions for adults working through anxiety, depression, grief, behavioral patterns, or significant life changes. Sessions are typically fifty minutes and held weekly or biweekly based on need.

Community Consultation

Organizational and institutional consultation services for local employers, civic groups, schools, and community programs. Dr. Voss has provided consultation to media organizations, municipal offices, and community health initiatives throughout the region.

Psychological Assessment

Formal evaluations for a range of clinical and vocational purposes. Assessments are conducted with care and precision, with written reports provided for referral or treatment planning.

Group Sessions

Dr. Voss periodically facilitates small group sessions for specific populations. Current group programming is on hiatus. Inquire about future availability.

Grief & Bereavement

Dedicated support for individuals navigating loss — including sudden loss, prolonged illness, disappearance, or unresolved mourning. Dr. Voss understands that grief rarely follows a predictable course.

Long-Term Cases

Dr. Voss accepts long-term therapeutic relationships for patients with complex histories. Some of his most enduring professional work has involved patients he has worked with across many years.

Community & Outreach

Rooted in Moth Hollow

"The health of a community is not measured by the absence of crisis — it is measured by what the community carries quietly, and for how long. My work has always been about listening to what goes unspoken."

— Dr. Elias Voss, address to the Moth Hollow Civic Forum, 1996

For many years, Dr. Voss extended his practice beyond individual sessions into the broader structures of Moth Hollow's community. He served as a consultant to local media, school programs, and health organizations — contributing to the psychological literacy and resilience of the region.

That outreach work concluded in 2001. Dr. Voss now focuses exclusively on private practice.

He remains a fixture of community life in Moth Hollow, where he has lived and worked for over thirty-five years.

Before Your First Session

Dr. Voss conducts a brief introductory consultation — typically twenty minutes — before scheduling ongoing sessions. This allows him to understand your situation and determine whether the practice is the right fit for your needs.

He works with a small patient roster by intention. This ensures that the attention he gives each person is undivided.

A Note on Privacy

All clinical records are maintained in strict confidence, in accordance with applicable law and professional ethics. Dr. Voss has maintained his own paper-based record system throughout his career. Electronic health records are not used by this practice.

Notice — Research Consultancy

Dr. Voss previously accepted consultancy requests from research institutions and independent investigators. This service is no longer offered. Inquiries of this nature will not be returned.
