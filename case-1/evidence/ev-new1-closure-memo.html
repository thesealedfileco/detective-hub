I. Purpose of this Memorandum

This memorandum serves as the official notice of disposition for missing persons case MHSD-98-0041, filed October 15, 1998, regarding the disappearance of Naomi Elise Vale (DOB: March 3, 1972), last known address 114 Ridgeline Road, Moth Hollow, WV 26250. This office hereby moves to close and archive the above-referenced case effective the date of this memorandum.

II. Summary of Investigation Conducted

The investigation was opened following a missing persons report filed by station manager R. Greer on October 15, 1998. The department conducted the following actions during the period October 15–25, 1998:

Action Item Status Notes
Initial scene walkthrough — WHLW station Complete No evidence of struggle. One unlisted archive item flagged. Playback review pending authorization — not completed.
Witness interview — E. Pike Complete Statement obtained Oct 16. Account accepted as filed.
Witness interview — R. Greer Complete Statement obtained Oct 17.
Relay tower perimeter search Complete Physical evidence recovered and logged. Referred to WVSP CID per protocol. CID report not yet received at time of closure.
Family notification & interview Complete G. Hart (brother) interviewed Oct 22. Next of kin notified.
Formal suspect interviews Incomplete Consulting station personnel not formally interviewed prior to closure. Voluntary interview conducted Oct 21 — notes filed separately.
Formal inquest / coroner referral N/A No remains recovered. Coroner referral not applicable at this time.
Reviewing commander sign-off Incomplete See Section IV.
III. Basis for Closure

After eleven days of active investigation, this office has determined that no evidence currently in our possession establishes the commission of a crime. The physical evidence recovered at the tower perimeter remains inconclusive pending the WVSP CID report, which has not been received. In the absence of a body, a crime scene determination, or a named suspect supported by material evidence, continued allocation of departmental resources to this case is not warranted at this time.

The case is closed as: OPEN/UNRESOLVED — INACTIVE. It may be reopened upon receipt of new material evidence or at the discretion of this office.
