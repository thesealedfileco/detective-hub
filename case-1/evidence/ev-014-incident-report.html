Narrative — officer account

On the evening of October 17, 1998, a search team was dispatched to the relay tower perimeter on Route 7 following an anonymous tip received by the department on October 16. The tip referenced activity at the tower on the night of October 14 — the date of Naomi Vale's disappearance.

Search team arrived on site at approximately 23:00. Conditions were poor — low visibility, no ambient light, ground wet from recent rainfall. A flashlight sweep of the tower perimeter identified several areas of interest including disturbed ground near the maintenance path, evidence of forced or unauthorized access at the tower gate, and physical items requiring collection.

One item of particular note was recovered from the left side of the access path approximately eight meters from the tower gate. See evidence inventory below.

The site is consistent with a location where a physical confrontation occurred. The nature and circumstances of that confrontation could not be established from physical evidence alone. Witness interviews were initiated — see page 2.

Evidence inventory — items recovered at scene
  • Blood traces — ground surface, tower gate perimeter — type A positive — consistent with Vale, Naomi E. per medical records on file
  • Flashlight — handheld, lens cracked, found approx. 4m from gate — identified as property of Vale, Naomi E. per personal inventory
  • Disturbed ground — area approx. 3m × 2m near maintenance path — consistent with signs of struggle
  • Broken fencing — barbed wire section on north perimeter — recently separated, not weather-related
  • Cassette tape casing — clear plastic, standard audio cassette size — found near gate post — no tape inside — casing only — contents absent
  • Partial footprint impression — cast taken — adult male, estimated size 10–11 — does not match Vale's footwear
  • Cigarette stub — ████ brand — collected for analysis
All items bagged and labeled on site. Full chain of custody documentation filed separately under case MH-1998-0014. Evidence bag reference: MH-7734.

Preliminary findings

The nature of the interaction between the two individuals cannot be formally established from the available record.
