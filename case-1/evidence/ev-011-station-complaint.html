Subject of report Unauthorized archive access and alteration of broadcast recordings
Report — in employee's own words

I am filing this report because I have been unable to get a response through informal channels and I believe the situation requires a formal record.

Over the past several months I have found tapes in the station archive that I did not place there and cannot account for. On at least three occasions I have also discovered that recordings I prepared for broadcast have been altered — material I do not recognize has been inserted into files I made myself. I have not been informed of any maintenance, editing, or archiving work that would explain these changes.

I have also found evidence that someone has been accessing the archive after hours. The door to the archive room has been unlocked on mornings when I was the last person to leave the night before and I know I locked it. The tape storage index has entries I did not make. I raised this with the evening engineer on two occasions. Nothing was done.

I want to be careful about what I write here because I am not certain, but I believe the person responsible may be Dr. Voss, the consulting psychologist attached to the Community Outreach Program. I have no proof. But his access to the station — including the archive — has never been clearly defined to me, and on one occasion I found him in the archive room at a time when he had no scheduled reason to be there. When I asked him about it he told me he was reviewing listener research materials. I do not know what that means in relation to the archive.

I am not making an accusation. I am making a record. I would like someone in management to look into this and tell me what Dr. Voss's access rights actually are and whether what I have described falls within them.

I would also like someone to listen to the altered recordings. I have kept one. I can provide it if asked.
